Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers "Publisher Canali", the internal publishing tool used by the creators of the science channels Quarks & Glue (English) and Quark & Colla (Italian), and this website. The tool is used only by the channel owners to manage their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool does

  • Uploads and schedules videos on the owners' own YouTube channels and publishes posts on their own Instagram accounts.
  • Reads public statistics of those videos and posts (views, likes, number of comments) to evaluate performance.
  • Reads comments left on those videos and posts, so the channel team can reply to viewers' questions. Replies may be drafted with the help of an AI assistant and are reviewed by the team.

Data we process

  • Account authorizations (OAuth tokens) for the owners' YouTube and Instagram accounts. They are stored only on the owners' computer and are never shared.
  • Public comments on our own videos and posts: the commenter's public display name and the comment text, used only to reply to that comment. We do not build profiles, sell data or use it for advertising.
  • To draft replies, the text of a comment and the description of the related video may be sent to an AI model provider (via OpenRouter). No account credentials are sent.
  • This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our channels you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the tool's access to a Google account at any time from the Google security settings page.

Instagram

The tool uses the Instagram API provided by Meta. Meta's processing is governed by the Instagram Data Policy.

Retention and deletion

Comment data is kept only as long as needed to manage replies (at most 12 months) and then deleted. To ask for deletion of any data related to you, write to the address below; we reply within 30 days.

Informativa privacy (sintesi in italiano)
